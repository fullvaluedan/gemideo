## 3. The Sabrina Ramonov Bookmark Cheat Sheet

### The Literal Copy-Paste Prompt:
```text
Record this workflow: 
1. Scan X for breaking AI news in the last 12 hours.
2. Filter for posts with engagement > 500 and verify against top 3 technical sources.
3. Draft a 3-bullet executive briefing email with citations.
4. Dispatch to team@company.com every morning at 06:00 AM.
```

### The $0 Free Software Stack:
1. **Grok on X:** Free tier / existing X subscription for real-time telemetry and task routing.
2. **Windows Steps Recorder / Chrome DevTools:** Free native tools to record clicks and generate macro coordinates.
3. **Gemini & Antigravity:** Free AI orchestration for prompt optimization and visual metaphor generation.
4. **CapCut Desktop (Free):** Automated 60-fps auto-captions with yellow/cyan keyword highlights and sub-bass audio leveling.
